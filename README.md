# Summary of Satellite Remote-Sensing Application Notes

> This repository contains 12 themed files with ~285 notes covering satellite remote-sensing applications from disaster response to urban monitoring, data processing, and international cooperation.

---

## 1. Fires & Hotspots (33 notes)

Focuses on wildfire, crop-residue burning, and industrial hotspot detection. Uses Suomi-NPP, Sentinel-2, Landsat and other multi-source data. Covers cases such as the Great Xing'an Range, California, and Australia bushfires, plus an operational land-surface hotspot monitoring system.

---

## 2. Floods & Water Disasters (26 notes)

All-weather flood monitoring with SAR satellites (Sentinel-1, GF-3). Includes the 2020 Chaohu Lake flood, southern China rainstorms, and urban waterlogging, emphasizing change detection and rapid WEBGIS publishing for disaster response.

---

## 3. Sea Ice & Snow (36 notes)

Arctic/Antarctic sea-ice trend analysis, glacier change, snow cover monitoring, and cold-region mapping. Uses Sentinel-1/2 and Landsat long-term time series, discussing differences between annual and multi-year composite products.

---

## 4. Water Quality & Water Bodies (11 notes)

Algal-bloom monitoring (e.g., Erhai Lake), eutrophication index analysis, and lake shrinkage (Chahan-Nur). Uses red-edge bands, MNDWI and other indices for multi-scale water-quality assessment from Sentinel-2 to Planet small-satellites.

---

## 5. Agriculture & Forestry (45 notes)

The largest theme. Covers crop-yield estimation, agricultural subsidy verification, forest logging monitoring, PV-station EIA, and protected-area remote-sensing training — the most livelihood-relevant application set for natural-resource management.

---

## 6. Nature Conservation & Ecology (6 notes)

World-heritage digital maps, Yarlung Tsangpo landslide-dam risk analysis, and ecological remote-sensing applications. Highlights the value of satellite remote sensing in transboundary ecological protection and heritage-site monitoring.

---

## 7. Nighttime Lights & Energy (24 notes)

NPP–SDGSAT combined night-light products, urban nighttime-light change, and energy-facility monitoring (nuclear and PV power stations). Explores experimental use of night-light data for economic assessment and national security.

---

## 8. Urban & Infrastructure (32 notes)

Urban expansion monitoring, illegal-construction remote-sensing inspection, high-resolution satellite evaluation (ZY1E, GF-2, SkySat), and road/construction-site tracking. Reflects the high-temporal-frequency needs of fine-scale urban management.

---

## 9. Oceans & Coasts (3 notes)

Ocean remote-sensing outreach curriculum, Sentinel-series introduction, and 30-year Bohai Sea reclamation change. Concise content aimed at marine remote-sensing education and macro-scale monitoring.

---

## 10. Disasters & Emergencies (7 notes)

Multi-source remote-sensing emergency response to the Jiuzhaigou earthquake, Jiangsu/Zhejiang chemical-plant explosion monitoring, and virtual ground-station instant WEB services. Demonstrates rapid satellite response from earthquake landslides to hazardous-chemical incidents.

---

## 11. International Geopolitics & Military (18 notes)

Planet image enhancement, promotion of Chinese satellite data in Central and Southeast Asia, SCO cooperation, Malaysia S&T cooperation project proposals, and the Suez Canal blockage. Shows the role of remote-sensing data in international cooperation and geopolitical analysis.

---

## 12. Data Processing & Algorithms (44 notes)

The second-largest theme. Covers the PixelKnife software toolchain, Cloudless World dynamic mapping, AI super-resolution, SAR change detection, image registration and fusion, and WEBGIS systems — the core remote-sensing data-processing technologies.

---

## Key Satellites & Data Sources

| Satellite | Characteristics |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Landsat 5/7/8 | Long time-series, 30 m multispectral, backbone for fire and land-change monitoring |
| Sentinel-1/2 | ESA free data, SAR all-weather + 10/20 m optical |
| GF-1/2/3/4/6 | China’s GF program, sub-meter optical and SAR |
| ZY1E | 2.5 m PAN-MS + hyperspectral |
| NPP / SDGSAT-1 | Night light, low-light and thermal infrared for socio-economic and energy monitoring |
| Planet Dove / SkySat | 3 m high-frequency small-sat constellation, 0.5 m high-resolution |
| SPOT5/6/7 | French high-resolution optical, historical archive and mapping |

## Background

The author is from the Aerospace Information Research Institute, Chinese Academy of Sciences (formerly the Institute of Remote Sensing and Digital Earth), with long-term experience in satellite remote-sensing data reception, processing, and application services. These notes derive from operational practice and science-communication writing, balancing technical depth with readability. Originally published on platforms such as WeChat Official Accounts.
