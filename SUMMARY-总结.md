# SatAppNotes — 卫星遥感应用笔记总结 / Summary of Satellite Remote-Sensing Application Notes

> 本仓库由 12 个主题文件组成，收录约 285 篇卫星遥感应用笔记，涵盖从灾害应急到城市监测、从数据处理到国际合作的广泛领域。
> This repository contains 12 themed files with ~285 notes covering satellite remote-sensing applications from disaster response to urban monitoring, data processing, and international cooperation.

---

## 1. 火灾与火点 / Fires & Hotspots (33 notes)

**中文**：聚焦林火、秸秆焚烧、工业高温点检测。涉及 Suomi-NPP、Sentinel-2、Landsat 等多源卫星数据，讲述大兴安岭、加州、澳洲等地的火情监测案例，以及业务化运行的地表高温点检测系统。

**English**: Focuses on wildfire, crop-residue burning, and industrial hotspot detection. Uses Suomi-NPP, Sentinel-2, Landsat and other multi-source data. Covers cases such as the Great Xing'an Range, California, and Australia bushfires, plus an operational land-surface hotspot monitoring system.

---

## 2. 洪涝与水患 / Floods & Water Disasters (26 notes)

**中文**：利用雷达卫星（Sentinel-1、GF-3）进行全天候洪涝监测。包含 2020 安徽巢湖洪涝、南方暴雨、城市内涝等案例，强调变化检测与 WEBGIS 快速发布在灾害响应中的作用。

**English**: All-weather flood monitoring with SAR satellites (Sentinel-1, GF-3). Includes the 2020 Chaohu Lake flood, southern China rainstorms, and urban waterlogging, emphasizing change detection and rapid WEBGIS publishing for disaster response.

---

## 3. 海冰与冰雪 / Sea Ice & Snow (36 notes)

**中文**：南北极海冰趋势分析、冰川变化、积雪监测，以及冰雪在寒区制图中的应用。涉及 Sentinel-1/2、Landsat 长期序列，讨论单年与多年合成产品的差异。

**English**: Arctic/Antarctic sea-ice trend analysis, glacier change, snow cover monitoring, and cold-region mapping. Uses Sentinel-1/2 and Landsat long-term time series, discussing differences between annual and multi-year composite products.

---

## 4. 水质与水体 / Water Quality & Water Bodies (11 notes)

**中文**：水华监测（如洱海）、富营养化指数分析、湖泊萎缩（察汗淖）等案例。利用红边波段、MNDWI 等指数，从 Sentinel-2 到 Planet 小卫星的多尺度水质评估。

**English**: Algal-bloom monitoring (e.g., Erhai Lake), eutrophication index analysis, and lake shrinkage (Chahan-Nur). Uses red-edge bands, MNDWI and other indices for multi-scale water-quality assessment from Sentinel-2 to Planet small-satellites.

---

## 5. 农业与林业 / Agriculture & Forestry (45 notes)

**中文**：面积最大的主题。覆盖作物估产、农业补贴核查、林区砍伐监测、光伏电站环评、自然保护区遥感培训等内容，是遥感面向自然资源管理最贴近民生的应用集合。

**English**: The largest theme. Covers crop-yield estimation, agricultural subsidy verification, forest logging monitoring, PV-station EIA, and protected-area remote-sensing training — the most livelihood-relevant application set for natural-resource management.

---

## 6. 自然保护与生态 / Nature Conservation & Ecology (6 notes)

**中文**：世界遗产电子地图、雅鲁藏布江堰塞湖风险分析、生态遥感应用等。强调卫星遥感在跨境生态保护与遗产地监测中的价值。

**English**: World-heritage digital maps, Yarlung Tsangpo landslide-dam risk analysis, and ecological remote-sensing applications. Highlights the value of satellite remote sensing in transboundary ecological protection and heritage-site monitoring.

---

## 7. 夜光与能源 / Nighttime Lights & Energy (24 notes)

**中文**：NPP 与 SDGSAT 联合夜光产品、城市夜间灯光变化、能源设施监测（核电站、光伏电站）。探索夜间灯光数据在经济活动评估与国家安全中的实验性应用。

**English**: NPP–SDGSAT combined night-light products, urban nighttime-light change, and energy-facility monitoring (nuclear and PV power stations). Explores experimental use of night-light data for economic assessment and national security.

---

## 8. 城市与基础设施 / Urban & Infrastructure (32 notes)

**中文**：城市扩张监测、违建（两违）遥感巡查、高分辨率卫星（ZY1E、GF-2、SkySat）评测、道路与建设工地跟踪。体现遥感在城市精细化管理中的高频次需求。

**English**: Urban expansion monitoring, illegal-construction remote-sensing inspection, high-resolution satellite evaluation (ZY1E, GF-2, SkySat), and road/construction-site tracking. Reflects the high-temporal-frequency needs of fine-scale urban management.

---

## 9. 海洋与海岸 / Oceans & Coasts (3 notes)

**中文**：海洋遥感科普课程大纲、哨兵系列专题介绍、渤海围填海三十年变迁。内容精炼，面向海洋遥感的科普与宏观监测。

**English**: Ocean remote-sensing outreach curriculum, Sentinel-series introduction, and 30-year Bohai Sea reclamation change. Concise content aimed at marine remote-sensing education and macro-scale monitoring.

---

## 10. 灾害事件与突发 / Disasters & Emergencies (7 notes)

**中文**：九寨沟地震多源遥感应急、江苏/浙江化工厂爆炸监测、虚拟地面站即时 WEB 服务等。展现从地震滑坡到危化事故的卫星快速响应能力。

**English**: Multi-source remote-sensing emergency response to the Jiuzhaigou earthquake, Jiangsu/Zhejiang chemical-plant explosion monitoring, and virtual ground-station instant WEB services. Demonstrates rapid satellite response from earthquake landslides to hazardous-chemical incidents.

---

## 11. 国际地缘与军事 / International Geopolitics & Military (18 notes)

**中文**：Planet 图像增强、中国卫星数据面向中亚与东南亚的推广、上合组织合作、马来西亚科技合作项目申请、苏伊士运河堵塞等。体现遥感数据在国际合作与地缘分析中的角色。

**English**: Planet image enhancement, promotion of Chinese satellite data in Central and Southeast Asia, SCO cooperation, Malaysia S&T cooperation project proposals, and the Suez Canal blockage. Shows the role of remote-sensing data in international cooperation and geopolitical analysis.

---

## 12. 数据处理与算法 / Data Processing & Algorithms (44 notes)

**中文**：第二大主题。涵盖 PixelKnife（像素刻刀）软件工具链、无云世界（Cloudless World）动态制图、AI 超分辨率、SAR 变化检测、图像配准与融合、WEBGIS 系统等核心遥感数据处理技术。

**English**: The second-largest theme. Covers the PixelKnife software toolchain, Cloudless World dynamic mapping, AI super-resolution, SAR change detection, image registration and fusion, and WEBGIS systems — the core remote-sensing data-processing technologies.

## 核心卫星与数据源 / Key Satellites & Data Sources

| 卫星 / Satellite | 特点 / Characteristics |
|-----------------|------------------------|
| Landsat 5/7/8 | 长时序、30 m 多光谱，火灾与地表变化监测的主力 / Long time-series, 30 m multispectral, backbone for fire and land-change monitoring |
| Sentinel-1/2 | 欧空局免费数据，SAR 全天候 + 10/20 m 光学 / ESA free data, SAR all-weather + 10/20 m optical |
| GF-1/2/3/4/6 | 中国高分专项，亚米级光学与雷达 / China’s GF program, sub-meter optical and SAR |
| ZY1E | 2.5 m 全色多光谱 + 高光谱 / 2.5 m PAN-MS + hyperspectral |
| NPP / SDGSAT-1 | 夜间灯光、微光与热红外，社会经济与能源监测 / Night light, low-light and thermal infrared for socio-economic and energy monitoring |
| Planet Dove / SkySat | 3 m 高频次小卫星星座、0.5 m 高分辨率 / 3 m high-frequency small-sat constellation, 0.5 m high-resolution |
| SPOT5/6/7 | 法国高分辨率光学卫星，历史档案与普查 / French high-resolution optical, historical archive and mapping |

## 背景说明 / Background

**中文**：笔记作者来自中国科学院空天信息创新研究院（原遥感与数字地球研究所），长期从事卫星遥感数据接收、处理与应用服务。内容源自业务实践与科普写作，兼顾技术深度与可读性，早期发布于微信公众号等平台。

**English**: The author is from the Aerospace Information Research Institute, Chinese Academy of Sciences (formerly the Institute of Remote Sensing and Digital Earth), with long-term experience in satellite remote-sensing data reception, processing, and application services. These notes derive from operational practice and science-communication writing, balancing technical depth with readability. Originally published on platforms such as WeChat Official Accounts.
